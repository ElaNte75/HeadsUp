# HeadsUp Auras

### Never miss the moment to press the right button.

In a fight it is easy to stare at one spot and miss what matters. **HeadsUp Auras** puts a clear icon on your screen at the exact moment you should act: Death Strike is ready and your health is low, Killing Machine lights up Obliterate, you finally have the runes or the Holy Power you were waiting for. When an ability lights up, it can also play a sound.

It is built for the **Midnight** rules, works for **every class and specialization**, and you make the icons yourself in a simple four-step window. No scripts, no strings to copy.

![The settings window](ADD-SCREENSHOT-1)

---

## Make your own icons in four steps

1. **Aura**: choose an ability, give it a name, pick its picture, size and place on the screen, and choose how it looks.
2. **Status**: your health, the enemy's health, whether the enemy is alive, and your resources.
3. **Ability**: whether it is available, its cooldown and charges, and whether it is **empowered**.
4. **Situation**: only in combat, out of combat, mounted, in a vehicle, and for which specializations.

The icon shows only when **all** your conditions are true. Hover over any option for a short explanation.

![Conditions](ADD-SCREENSHOT-2)

## What it can do

**Smart conditions**
- Your health and the enemy's health, with *below, equal or below, equal, equal or above, above*.
- **Resources that match your class**: Runic Power and Runes, Mana and Holy Power, Rage, Energy, Combo Points, Soul Shards, Chi, Essence and more.
- **Available**: the ability can be used right now (not on cooldown, and you have enough resource).
- **Cooldown and charges**, and a **hide time** after you cast the ability.
- **Empowered abilities**: HeadsUp Auras knows which abilities can light up (proc glows) for every class. Choose "ignore the other conditions" and the icon shows whenever the ability lights up, whatever else is true.

**Make it impossible to miss**
- Three eye-catching effects for empowered abilities: **Sunburst**, **Flame** and **Burst**, plus a glow or pulse for any icon.
- **A sound** the moment an ability lights up: six sounds of our own and eight of the game, in three volumes.

![Effects](ADD-SCREENSHOT-3)

**Yours, exactly the way you want**
- **Any icon from the game**, any size (width and height, linked or separate).
- **Place it exactly**: drag it with the mouse, or type the X and Y position (it never leaves your screen).
- **Display priority** when icons overlap, and keep an icon on screen for a few seconds after combat.
- **Many auras, tidy list**: deactivate the ones you do not need now without deleting them, **duplicate** any aura, and activate them again later.
- A **button on the minimap** opens and closes the window.

## Getting started

1. Type `/hua`, or click the HeadsUp Auras button on the minimap.
2. Choose an ability, set a condition or two, press **Save aura**.
3. Drag the icon where you want it.

## Good to know

Since patch 12.0 the game hides some values from add-ons during combat (your health, your main resource, cooldowns). HeadsUp Auras is built for that: it shows icons for those conditions without ever reading the hidden values. Because of the same rule, the **sound** works only where the game lets an add-on know what is happening, which includes abilities lighting up, your second resource (runes, Holy Power, combo points...) and the enemy being alive. We would rather tell you than promise what the game does not allow.
