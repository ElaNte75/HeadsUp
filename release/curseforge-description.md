# HeadsUp

**Never miss the moment to press the right button.**

In a fight it is easy to stare at one spot and miss what matters. **HeadsUp** puts a clear icon on your screen at the exact moment you should act: when Death Strike is ready and your health is low, when Killing Machine lights up Obliterate, when you have the runes you need. It replaces what you used to do with custom auras, built for the new Midnight rules, and it works for **every class and specialization**.

![screenshot: the window](PUT-A-SCREENSHOT-OF-THE-WINDOW-HERE)

## How it works

You build your own icons in a simple four-step window:

1. **Aura**: choose an ability, give it a name, choose its picture and size, and place it on the screen.
2. **Conditions**: your health, the enemy's health, whether the enemy is alive, and your resources.
3. **Ability**: whether it is available, its cooldown and charges, and whether it is **empowered**.
4. **Situation**: only in combat, out of combat, mounted, in a vehicle, and for which specializations.

The icon shows only when **all** your conditions are true. Hover over any option to see a short explanation.

## Features

- **Resources that match your class**: Runic Power and Runes, Mana and Holy Power, Rage, Energy, Combo Points, Soul Shards, Chi, Essence and more. Compare with below, equal or below, equal, equal or above, above.
- **Empowered abilities**: knows which abilities can light up (proc glows) for every class, and shows you the buff that does it. One option shows the icon *whenever* the ability is empowered, whatever the other conditions say.
- **Eye-catching effects** for empowered abilities: **Sunburst**, **Flame** and **Burst**, plus a glow or pulse for any icon.
- **Place it exactly**: drag it with the mouse, or type the X and Y position (with limits so it never leaves the screen).
- **Any size**: width and height, linked or separate.
- **Keep it after combat** for a few seconds, **hide it after you cast** the ability, and a **display priority** when icons overlap.
- **Many auras, tidy list**: hide the ones you do not need now without deleting them, and bring them back later.
- **Short explanations everywhere**: a tooltip on every option.

## Getting started

1. Type `/headsup`.
2. Choose an ability, set a condition or two, press **Save aura**.
3. Drag the icon where you want it.
